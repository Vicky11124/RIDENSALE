Best Second-Hand Bike Models to Sell in Chennai

Buying a second-hand bike in Chennai is a smart way to beat the city's notorious traffic while keeping your daily commute cost-effective. However, finding the right pre-owned motorcycle that offers both reliability and good fuel efficiency requires careful research.

Whether you're a college student looking for an affordable commuter, a working professional needing a durable ride, or an enthusiast seeking a performance motorcycle, this guide compares the best second-hand bikes in Chennai based on market demand, durability, maintenance cost, and typical resale values.

1. The Best Commuter Bikes (100cc - 125cc)

If fuel economy and low maintenance are your top priorities, these commuter models are the undisputed champions in the Tamil Nadu market.

Honda Shine (125cc)

  • Why buy it: Exceptionally smooth engine, comfortable seating posture, and excellent mileage of 55-60 kmpl in heavy traffic.
  • Expected Resale Price (2-5 years old): ₹40,000 - ₹65,000

Hero Splendor Plus (100cc)

  • Why buy it: Legendary reliability, cheap spare parts, and incredible fuel efficiency of 65+ kmpl. It is highly valued in both Chennai and sub-urban areas.
  • Expected Resale Price (2-5 years old): ₹30,000 - ₹50,000

2. The Best Premium & Sports Commuters (150cc - 200cc)

For riders seeking a balance between daily commuting comfort and aggressive styling, the 150cc-200cc class is the fastest-selling category in Chennai.

TVS Apache RTR 160 4V

  • Why buy it: Loaded with features like riding modes, great pick-up, sharp handling, and a sporty engine. Spars and services are easily accessible in Chennai.
  • Expected Resale Price (2-5 years old): ₹60,000 - ₹90,000

Yamaha FZ-S FI (150cc)

  • Why buy it: Known for its muscular look, wide rear tire (great road grip on wet Chennai roads), fuel injection stability, and long engine lifespan.
  • Expected Resale Price (2-5 years old): ₹55,000 - ₹85,000

3. The Cruiser King (350cc+)

Chennai has a massive community of touring and cruising enthusiasts. One brand completely dominates this sector.

Royal Enfield Classic 350

  • Why buy it: High brand value, solid metal build, iconic exhaust beat, and comfortable cruising speed. Royal Enfield bikes hold their resale value extremely well.
  • Expected Resale Price (2-5 years old): ₹1,20,000 - ₹1,80,000
"Royal Enfield Classic 350 models typically retain up to 70% of their original showroom value even after 3 years of usage, making them a highly secure pre-owned investment."

Essential Second Hand Bike Buying Checklist

Before handing over your hard-earned money to a private seller or used motorcycle dealer in Chennai, make sure to run through these vital verification steps:

  1. Verify Odometer Reading: Look for signs of tampering (scratches on the dial, misaligned digits). Check service records to match the kilometers driven.
  2. Engine Sound & Exhaust: Start the engine when cold. Listen for abnormal tapping or metallic rattling sounds. Heavy white or blue smoke from the exhaust indicates worn-out piston rings.
  3. Document Authenticity: Ensure the engine number and chassis number match the RC (Registration Certificate). Check for active hypothecation (loan) — a clean sale requires a bank NOC.
  4. Check Active Fines: Search the vehicle plate number on the digital Vahan portal or traffic portal to ensure there are no unpaid challans or legal disputes pending.
