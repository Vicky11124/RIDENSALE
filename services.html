STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

HOW TO SELL YOUR USED BIKE

From valuation and inspection to payment and RC transfer, here's how RideNSale makes selling your bike simple from start to finish.

01

FREE BIKE VALUATION

Tell us your bike's make, model, year and kilometres to get started with a valuation based on its condition and current market demand.

02

FREE DOORSTEP BIKE INSPECTION

Our evaluator visits your preferred location to inspect the bike's condition, documents and key components before preparing the final offer.

03

LOAN & FINANCE SUPPORT

If your bike is still financed, we'll guide you through the loan closure and required documentation so you can complete the sale smoothly.

04

GET YOUR FINAL OFFER

After the inspection, we provide an offer based on your bike's condition, market value and other relevant factors. Review it and decide whether you'd like to proceed.

05

SECURE PAYMENT

Once the sale is finalized, payment is processed securely through bank transfer according to the agreed terms (typically within 30 minutes to 2 hours).

06

RC TRANSFER SUPPORT

We help with the required ownership-transfer paperwork and RTO formalities after the sale.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

WHAT DO I NEED TO SELL MY BIKE?

  • RC (Registration Certificate)
  • Valid ID proof (Aadhaar / PAN)
  • Valid Insurance Policy
  • Pollution Certificate (PUC), where applicable
  • Loan NOC / Foreclosure statement (if financed)
  • Other ownership documents required for transfer

Don't have every document ready? Contact RideNSale and we'll guide you through what's required for your situation.

We handle bike valuations, doorstep inspections, loan closures, secure payments, and RC transfers at no extra cost. Every step is designed to make selling your bike simple and stress-free.
