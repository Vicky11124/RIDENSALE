Step-by-Step RTO RC Transfer Guide in Chennai

When you buy or sell a used vehicle, the transaction is legally complete only when the Registration Certificate (RC) is transferred to the new owner's name. In Chennai, the Regional Transport Office (RTO) manages this process. Failing to transfer the RC is a massive liability — if the new rider gets into an accident or commits a traffic violation, the original owner is still legally responsible.

This guide explains the step-by-step RC Transfer process in Chennai, covering all mandatory RTO forms, fees, and required documents.

1. The Mandatory Documents Checklist

Before submitting your application at the RTO, gather these crucial files:

  • Original RC: The registration card or book of the vehicle.
  • Form 29 & Form 30: The official forms for transfer of ownership. Must be signed by both buyer and seller.
  • Valid Insurance Certificate: Copy of the active insurance policy in the buyer's name.
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) Certificate: Copy of the emission certificate.
  • ID & Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or Passport of both parties.
  • Chassis Print: A pencil print of the bike's chassis number on a plain piece of paper.

2. Understanding the RTO Forms

Different forms are required based on the nature of the transaction:

Form 29 (Notice of Transfer of Ownership)

This form acts as a declaration that the seller has handed over all vehicle documents and physical possession to the buyer.

Form 30 (Report of Transfer of Ownership)

This is the formal application to the registering authority request to transfer the ownership name to the buyer.

Form 28 (No Objection Certificate - NOC)

If you are selling a bike registered in one RTO zone (e.g., RTO Tambaram TN-11) to a buyer residing in another RTO zone (e.g., RTO Anna Nagar TN-02), you must first obtain an NOC using Form 28 from the original registering RTO.

3. Step-by-Step Process (Online & Physical Submission)

  1. Online Application: Visit the official Parivahan Sewa portal. Enter the registration number, select "Transfer of Ownership," and fill out the details.
  2. Fee Payment: Pay the RTO transfer fee online (usually ₹150 for motorcycles and ₹300-₹500 for cars, plus postal charges). Print the receipt.
  3. Physical RTO Submission: Put all forms, online receipts, chassis print, and original RC into a folder. Submit them to the clerk at the RTO under whose jurisdiction the buyer resides.
  4. Verification: The RTO inspector verifies the chassis print and original document signatures.
  5. New RC Dispatch: Once verified, the new RC card is printed and sent to the buyer's registered address via Speed Post within 3 to 4 weeks.
"Under the Motor Vehicles Act, the RTO must be notified of the ownership transfer within 14 days of the physical vehicle sale."
