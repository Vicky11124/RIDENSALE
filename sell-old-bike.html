VALUATION FACTORS

WHAT DETERMINES AN OLD BIKE'S RESALE VALUE?

Engine & Gearbox Health

A smooth-running engine, healthy gearbox, and absence of smoke or unusual noise can significantly improve your bike's resale value.

Total Odometer Mileage

Lower mileage generally attracts more buyers and can help you receive a stronger resale offer.

RTO Registration & FC Status

Valid registration, up-to-date FC status, and clear RTO records make the bike easier to sell and can improve its valuation.

PAPERWORK GUIDE

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED TO SELL AN OLD BIKE

Depending on your vehicle transaction and financing status, documents typically include:

  • Original RC / Registration Certificate Smartcard
  • Valid Insurance Policy (if active)
  • Valid PUC (Pollution Under Control) Certificate
  • Government ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / PAN Card)
  • Address Proof (if required for RTO transfer)
  • Loan NOC & Form 35 (if bike was financed)
